About Me

My name is Bilal Mohammed, 1998. Bachelor's degree in Media and was among the top graduates in my department. I am interested in content creation and in bringing a unique style to cinematography and color grading. I have received several accredited certificates related to my field, including one from Al Jazeera in sound, another from Kurdish journalism, and a certificate for ranking first in my department and among the top students at Cihan University in Erbil. I have experience in video shooting, color grading, and editing, with a tendency toward cinematic style.
